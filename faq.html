Frequently Asked Questions

Vows of Love | International Wedding Filmmakers & Photographers

1. What makes Vows of Love different?

Our approach is rooted in documentary storytelling. We focus on genuine emotions, natural moments and meaningful connections, creating timeless photographs and cinematic films that preserve how your wedding truly felt.

2. Where is Vows of Love based?

We are based in Kochi, Kerala, and photograph and film weddings across India and worldwide.

3. Do you cover destination and international weddings?

Absolutely! We specialise in destination and curated weddings across India and international locations. Travel, accommodation and other destination-related expenses are discussed separately.

4. Do you cover weddings of different cultures and traditions?

Yes. We document Christian, Hindu, multicultural and other wedding celebrations, adapting our storytelling approach to the traditions and personalities of each couple.

5. Do you offer both photography and filmmaking?

Yes. We offer documentary wedding photography, cinematic highlight films, full wedding documentary films, pre-wedding stories and social media edits.

6. Will you guide us during the photoshoot?

Of course! While our style is documentary-driven, we provide gentle direction when needed, particularly for couple portraits and family photographs. We want you to feel comfortable and enjoy your wedding naturally.

7. How many photographers and filmmakers will cover our wedding?

Our team size depends on the scale, schedule and requirements of your celebration. We recommend a suitable team based on your selected collection.

8. Can we customise our wedding collection?

Yes. Every wedding is unique. We can customise your collection based on the number of events, coverage hours, team size, locations and deliverables.

9. How do we confirm our booking?

A 30% advance payment is required to reserve your wedding date. Your booking is confirmed once the advance is received and the booking terms are agreed upon.

10. What is your cancellation policy?

The 30% booking advance is non-refundable in the event of cancellation by the client. Date changes are subject to our availability and agreed postponement terms.

11. When will we receive our photographs and films?

Our standard delivery timelines are:

  • Edited photographs: 1–2 weeks
  • Wedding films: 4–7 weeks
  • Documentary films: approximately 45 days

The final timelines will be confirmed in your Shoot Design Document.

12. How will we receive our wedding photographs?

Your edited photographs will be delivered through a premium online gallery, making it easy to view, download and share your wedding memories with family and friends.

13. Does your online gallery have AI face search?

Yes! Our online galleries feature AI-powered face search, allowing you and your guests to find photographs of yourselves using a reference photo. It’s a convenient way to discover personal moments without scrolling through the entire wedding gallery.

14. How long will our online gallery be available?

Our standard online gallery is available for one year, unless otherwise specified in your selected collection. We recommend downloading and securely backing up your photographs.

15. Do you provide RAW photographs?

RAW photographs can be provided when specifically included or agreed upon in your selected collection.

16. Do you provide printed wedding albums?

Yes. Premium wedding albums are available as an optional addition. Once the album layout is approved, printing and delivery generally take approximately 15 days.

17. Do you work with wedding planners?

Absolutely. We collaborate with wedding planners, venues and creative teams to coordinate schedules and ensure a seamless photography and filmmaking experience.

18. How far in advance should we book?

We recommend contacting us as early as possible, especially for destination and international weddings, as popular wedding dates tend to fill up quickly.
