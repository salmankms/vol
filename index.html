Our Approach

We strongly believe that, genuine interest of understanding each of our couple is the best way to ensure the documentation of your big day and to deliver something that stays close to their heart forever. Our approach involves three stages,

We'll start with Pre-Production and planning, creating a customized pre-design document outlining the rituals, events, and timelines for your day. This helps with time management. The Creative Curator leads the process, understanding your needs and coordinating with the team, including facilitating testimonials from your recommended people. A Team Associate will be your point of contact throughout the process.

The Execution, our Creative Curator with a team of experts in visual documenting will be blending with the you both in a great way of understanding your tastes on photography. Our Lead Photographers and Videographers will be assigned with specific roles and responsibilities to ensure the best way of creative documenting. Along with that, CC helps with guidance in your whole from visual creators perspective.

The Postproduction, is where we presents the everlasting visual outputs ensuring every timelines which is already shared with you. Here we ensures your preferences with freedom of changes in outputs with our Creative Curator and Lead Editor.
