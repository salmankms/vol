About VOL

Founded in 2021, VOL (Vows of Love) was brought to life by a group of passionate creatives with over 10 years of experience in photography and visual storytelling.

Our mission is simple — to beautifully document your memories, personalities, and unique story in the most authentic way possible. We focus on capturing raw emotions, genuine connections, and real moments that you’ll cherish for a lifetime.

At VOL, we believe weddings are not staged events — they are emotional stories filled with love, people, energy, and unforgettable memories. Our approach is deeply documentary-driven, cinematic, and authentic.

About Vows of Love

Founded in 2021, Vows of Love was created by Jees and Emi with a shared passion for photography, filmmaking and visual storytelling.

With over a decade of experience in the wedding and visual storytelling space, we document celebrations through an approach that is honest, emotional and deeply personal.

For us, weddings are not staged events. They are stories unfolding in real time — filled with people, relationships, energy, anticipation, laughter, tears and fleeting moments.

Our job is to observe, anticipate and preserve those moments as honestly as possible.

We believe the most meaningful photographs and films aren’t always the perfectly posed ones. They are the moments that take you back to exactly how you felt.

Our Approach

RAW

Unfiltered emotions. Honest moments. Real stories.

We allow moments to unfold naturally rather than turning your wedding into a constant photoshoot.

PURE

Timeless imagery built around people and connection.

We look beyond the décor and details to document the relationships, emotions and atmosphere that make your wedding yours.

CHOSEN

Every frame has a reason.

From thousands of moments, we carefully choose the images and scenes that deserve to become part of your story.

Where We Work

Based in Kochi, Kerala, we photograph and film weddings across India and worldwide.

From beautifully curated celebrations in Kerala to destination weddings across India and international celebrations, we adapt our storytelling to different cultures, locations and wedding experiences while keeping our documentary approach at the heart of everything we do.

Kerala • India • Worldwide

Experience & Collaborations

Over the years, Vows of Love has worked alongside wedding planners, venues, creative teams and couples across India and international destinations.

We understand that a wedding is a collaboration between many people. Our team works calmly and professionally alongside planners and other creative teams, allowing the celebration to unfold while we document it without becoming intrusive.
